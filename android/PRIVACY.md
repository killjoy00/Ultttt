# Ultimate Tic/Tac/Toe for Android — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 19, 2026**

This policy describes the **Android offline test version** of Ultimate Tic/Tac/Toe (package `com.killjoy00.ultttt`). The iPhone app has a different privacy policy because it offers Apple Game Center and advertising-related functionality.

## Information collected or shared by the Android app

The initial Android version does **not** ask you to register, create an account, provide contact details, or connect to a server. It does not integrate advertising, analytics, online matchmaking, payment processing, or Google Play Games sign-in. The Android application itself does not request internet access.

The developer does not collect or sell personal data through this Android version and does not share your game history with third parties.

## On-device game data

Game moves, current match, local win totals, settings, and preferences are stored in your device's private app storage. This allows play to continue when you return to the app. Data is saved locally, not on a developer-operated backend. Depending on your Android device and backup settings, the operating system may back up eligible app data; those backups are controlled by your device platform and account, not by this developer.

You may erase local game data by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android Settings.

## Permissions, advertising, and purchases

This Android build does not request location, contacts, microphone, camera, notification, or internet permissions. There are no advertisements or in-app purchases in this build. No Google advertising SDKs are included in the Android application.

## Children and families

The game contains a strategy board game without in-app chat or user-generated content. Parents and guardians may manage device usage via Android's parental controls. The game does not request children's personal information.

## Future versions

If future Android versions add online multiplayer, Google Play Games, advertisements, purchases, analytics, or other network services, this policy and the Google Play Data safety declarations must be updated **before** those features are released.

## Questions

For support or privacy inquiries, see [the project support page](https://killjoy00.github.io/Ultttt/support/) or [open a GitHub issue](https://github.com/killjoy00/Ultttt/issues). Avoid posting personal or sensitive details in public issues.
