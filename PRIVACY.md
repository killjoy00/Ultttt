# Ultimate Tic Tac Toe — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 16, 2026**

Ultimate Tic Tac Toe is an iPhone game. This policy describes information handled by the app and the third-party services it uses.

## Game progress

Local games, game history, scores, settings, and statistics are stored on your device. The developer does not operate a separate game account or analytics server. Deleting the app deletes its on-device saved data, subject to any device backups you maintain.

## Apple Game Center

If you choose online play, achievements, or leaderboards, Apple Game Center handles your Apple gaming identity, online matches and turns, achievements, and leaderboard scores. Apple's terms and privacy policy govern Game Center information. Game Center can send turn notifications according to your Apple Account and device settings.

## Purchases

The optional Remove Ads upgrade uses Apple's StoreKit. Apple handles payment details and validates purchases. The game receives verified purchase entitlements, not your complete payment card information.

## Advertising and privacy choices

The free game integrates Google AdMob advertising and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for consent and privacy choices. Depending on your region, consent choices and SDK configuration, these services may process device and app information, identifiers, advertising interactions, and approximate location information to serve, measure, or protect ads. Ad requests are made only when Google's consent SDK reports they are allowed.

For Google's practices see https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://business.safety.google/privacy/.

If a verified Remove Ads purchase is active, the game does not request banner ads. Where required, you can access advertising privacy choices in the game's purchases/privacy interface.

## Notifications

Game Center provides online turn notifications. Ultimate Tic Tac Toe does not operate its own notification service.

## Information retention and removal

Saved local game history is under your control and can be removed by deleting the app. Game Center and Apple purchase records are managed by Apple; advertising and consent data is managed by Google according to their own policies and your choices.

The developer does not sell its local game-history records to outside companies. No separate developer-controlled backend is used to store your matches or personal information.

## Families

Ultimate Tic Tac Toe is a general-audience strategy game. Apple Game Center and Google advertising are subject to their own age and eligibility requirements. Parents can manage Game Center interactions and other app settings through Apple's parental controls.

## Contact

For support and privacy inquiries use https://github.com/killjoy00/Ultttt/issues. **Do not include sensitive personal information in public GitHub issues.**

The developer may update this policy when game features or third-party services change. The App Store's published privacy disclosures will reflect the final production SDK configuration.
